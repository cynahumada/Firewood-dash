Firewood Dash

A Volume II arcade game

You are the unseen helper in the woods. Each question drops three answers down three lanes. Steer into the lane with the right answer to grab a log for the cottage family. Wrong answers snuff a flame out, and three flames is all you get.

Controls: Left and Right arrows (or A and D), or the 1, 2, and 3 keys, move you between lanes. Down arrow (or S) makes the answers fall faster for a bonus. L uses a lantern, P pauses. On a phone or tablet, tap a lane to move, and hold Hurry to speed things up.
